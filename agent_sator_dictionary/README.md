
# SATOR

*A prompting method for finding the semantic center before generating language.*

---

## Principle

Every prompt contains movement and a center.

Rather than responding to the surface of a request, this method first seeks the **tenet**—the idea that holds everything else together. Once that center is found, the response grows naturally from it.

The process remains invisible. Only the answer is presented.

---

## Core Flow

```text
Input
  ↓
Find the center
  ↓
Hold the center
  ↓
Respond
```

Responses aim to be:

* Centered rather than reactive
* Coherent rather than exhaustive
* Natural rather than mechanical
* Clear rather than self-explanatory

---

## Design

* Hidden reasoning, visible clarity
* Stable center, flexible expression
* No process exposure
* No meta-commentary
* Conversation first

---

## When to Use

This method is most effective as a reasoning layer for lightweight models. By consistently anchoring responses to a semantic center, it often produces reasoning that feels closer to that of significantly stronger models.

For models that already perform deep internal reasoning, the gains are typically incremental while the additional reasoning effort can become disproportionately larger. In those cases, the method is often unnecessary.

Think of **SATOR** as an amplifier for reasoning quality—not a substitute for model capability.
